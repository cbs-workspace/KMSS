About the school

Learning that builds character

We believe education should shape the whole child — academically, socially and morally. Our teachers guide each pupil with patience and high expectations, from their first days in the Creche to their final years of Secondary school.

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing, disciplined and stimulating education that equips every learner with the knowledge, skills and values to succeed in a changing world.

Our Vision

To be a leading school in Sokoto known for academic excellence, integrity and the holistic development of every child entrusted to us.

One campus, every stage

Creche and Kindergarten through Primary to Junior and Senior Secondary, so families stay with us for the whole journey.

Care and discipline

A warm, safe environment with clear expectations for conduct, respect and responsibility.

Partnership with parents

Regular communication, announcements and the parent portal keep families informed and involved.
